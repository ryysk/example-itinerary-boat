Mobility & Accessibility Reminders

Bathroom Step: Standard Category 4C interior cabins have an approximate 4 to 8 inch raised threshold step into the bathroom. Verify if this meets traveler mobility needs.

Terminal Assistance: Wheelchair escort is available from check-in through security to the ship gangway. For full-cruise mobility, bring a personal folding transport chair or rent one delivered to the cabin.

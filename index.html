Stateroom Accessibility Notice
Standard Category 4C interior cabins have a raised bathroom threshold step (approx. 4 to 8 inches) and doorways around 22–24 inches[cite: 1]. Verify that this sill is safe for all senior travelers.
Terminal Wheelchair Assistance
Carnival provides terminal boarding escorts upon arrival. Drop bags and guests at curbside porters before proceeding to the 810 Channelside garage[cite: 1]. For shipboard mobility, bring a personal folding transport chair or rent one to the cabin.
